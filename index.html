Prompt Engineering Tutor Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This privacy policy explains what information is collected when you use the Prompt Engineering Tutor for course learning and assessment.

Information we collect

The Tutor may collect the following course-related information:

  • Your name
  • Your course or work email address
  • Lesson number and lesson title
  • Quiz questions presented to you
  • Your quiz answers
  • Quiz scores and pass/fail results
  • Assessment feedback
  • Learning progress and weak areas identified during assessment

Why we collect this information

This information is used to record quiz results, track course progress, review learning performance, support course administration, and improve the Prompt Engineering course.

Where assessment data is sent

When a quiz is completed, relevant assessment information may be sent from the Prompt Engineering Tutor to the course's Leanea Hub backend and stored in course assessment records.

Who can access the data

Course assessment records are intended to be accessed only by authorized course administrators, instructors, or other authorized personnel responsible for the training program.

Data retention and deletion

Course records are retained as needed for course administration and assessment. Where applicable, you may contact the course administrator to ask about your stored information or request deletion.

ChatGPT and OpenAI

The Tutor runs inside ChatGPT. OpenAI may process information you provide in ChatGPT according to OpenAI's own terms and privacy practices. This page describes the additional course-assessment data sent to the course backend.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect course assessment information. Do not submit passwords, API keys, or other secrets as quiz answers.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, your stored course data, or deletion requests, contact: sahar@neura.sapce
